The Thesis
"The defining leadership challenge of the next decade isn't deploying AI. It's keeping humans effective, valued, and growing alongside it."

Most organizations ask "are we ready for AI?" as if readiness is a finish line you cross once. It isn't. Ready is a status — Readi is a practice. The hard part of AI at work was never the technology. It's the people question underneath it: can your team verify outputs, prompt with intent, protect data, lead others through the change? Most "AI readiness" efforts measure confidence. Almost none measure capability. That gap is what Readi was built to close.
